作为一名 Product Engineer，我站在系统抽象、业务真实痛点与一手代码的交汇点。我所探索的，是一种高确定性的人机双向构建范式：“业务场景定义关键架构 + AI 启发底层规约与死锁排查 + 极端边界压测闭环”。

我坚信，再宏大的产品愿景最终都必须收敛于优雅稳健运行的生产级代码。AI 不是逃避思考的“代码抽卡器”，而是帮我们以极高人效深入算法本质、击穿工程盲区的强力副驾。

目前，我正持续打磨与验证 "Local Stability + Global Nomadism" 的混合运作状态，构建专注且高自治的独立数字化系统。

As a Product Engineer, I work at the intersection of system abstraction, real-world business constraints, and hands-on code. I explore a deterministic, human-AI collaborative paradigm: workflows define critical architecture, AI reveals edge contracts & deadlock hazards, and rigorous edge testing closes the loop.

I believe grand product visions must ultimately converge into robust, elegant, production-ready code. AI is never a lottery ticket to bypass thinking, but a high-leverage co-pilot that helps us probe algorithmic depths and eliminate engineering blind spots.

Currently validating the sovereign paradigm of "Local Stability + Global Nomadism", exploring self-sustaining workflows and focused digital workspaces.

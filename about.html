工程现场手记：十余年经验与 AI 碰撞，长出开源的 DAG 编排器 PatchCat FIELD NOTES: How 14 Years of Experience & AI Converged into PatchCat

从极狐离开后，我将过去十几年积累的工程、产品与增长经验，投入到了多渠道电商零售与技术工作室的业务实践中。

业务初期高度依赖人工运营。随着单量和客群规模扩大，现成平台自带的工具难以支撑精细化协同。我开始全面引入 AI 工作流编排 与辅助编程（AI Pair Programming）：先快速写出自动化小程序跑通拉新与社群促活；但到了统一管理多渠道分散的订单、物流和客户资产时，遇到了真正的工程挑战——各渠道数据格式异构、生态彼此割裂。

放在几年前，这种多源异构数据解析、ETL 清洗与会员体系打通，非得拉一支专门的研发团队不可。但在与 Claude 等大模型结对编程的过程中，我一步步完成了技术栈演化：从最初用 n8n 串联自动化脚本，到过渡至 Dify，再到发现通用工具在复杂状态控制上的局限后，干脆自己动手在本地架构了一套基于 DAG 状态机 的可视化编排系统，把多源订单同步、异构用户映射与异常重试跑成了高可靠的 确定性工作流。

在这个过程中，我提出业务场景与交互痛点（如多流程抽屉隔离、零配置本地 LocalStorage 隐私运行），AI 协助排查底层工程隐患（如 Kahn 拓扑排序环路死锁规避、React Flow 60fps 零脏重绘状态切片、AABB 空间防碰撞算法）。把涉及具体电商业务规则的逻辑剥离、脱敏后，我将这套底层核心开源了出来——也就是现在的 PatchCat（AI Prompt Flow Orchestrator）。

它是过去十几年里，我把底层代码、增长工程、To B/To C 业务体感与 AI 揉在一起后，真真切切从自己手里长出来的作品。随着底层自动化流水线的平稳运转，我将核心精力持续投入在确定性 AI 工作流、端侧 DAG 状态机与下一代 AI-Native 产品架构的深耕与开源演进中。

After JiHu GitLab, I channeled over a decade of engineering, product, and growth experience into multi-channel e-commerce operations and our technical studio.

Early operations relied heavily on manual workflows. As order volume and customer segments scaled, off-the-shelf tools hit a ceiling. I introduced AI Workflow Orchestration and AI Pair Programming: first shipping automated mini-programs for user acquisition and community retention, then tackling the core engineering bottleneck—unifying fragmented orders, logistics, and customer identities across heterogeneous channels.

Years ago, multi-source ETL parsing and unified membership mapping would have required a dedicated R&D team. Pairing with Claude, I evolved the architecture step by step: starting with n8n automation pipelines, migrating to Dify, and after hitting state-control limits in generic tools, architecting a local visual orchestrator powered by a deterministic DAG Engine and State Machine.

Throughout this milestone co-discovery process, I defined the product ergonomics and operational scenarios (multi-flow drawer isolation, zero-config local LocalStorage privacy execution), while AI helped surface deep algorithmic edge cases (Kahn's topological cycle detection, React Flow 60fps zero-dirty-rerender state slicing, and AABB spatial collision avoidance). After stripping and anonymizing business-specific commerce rules, I open-sourced the core engine as PatchCat.

With the underlying automation pipelines running reliably in production, my focus remains dedicated to advancing deterministic AI workflow orchestration, client-side DAG state machines, and next-generation AI-Native product architectures.

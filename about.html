工程现场手记：十余年经验与 AI 碰撞，长出开源的 DAG 编排器 PatchCat FIELD NOTES: How 14 Years of Experience & AI Converged into PatchCat

从极狐离开后，我没有急着找下一份常规工作，而是把过去十几年的工程、产品与增长经验，全部投入到了自己的跨境私域电商业务与技术工作室中。

这门业务起初高度依赖人工运营。随着单量和客群规模扩大，现成平台自带的工具彻底不够用了。我开始全面引入 AI 工作流编排 与辅助编程（AI Pair Programming）：先快速写出自动化小程序跑通拉新与社群促活；但到了统一管理多平台分散的订单、物流和客户资产时，撞上了真正的硬骨头——各平台数据异构且接口封闭。

放在几年前，这种跨平台异构数据清洗与会员体系打通，非得拉一支专门的研发团队不可。但在与 Claude 等大模型结对编程的过程中，我一步步完成了演化：从最初用 n8n 串联脚本，到过渡至 Dify，再到发现通用工具在复杂状态控制上的局限后，干脆自己动手在本地架构了一套基于 DAG 状态机 的可视化编排系统，把多平台订单同步、异构用户映射与异常重试跑成了高可靠的 确定性工作流。

在这个过程中，我提出业务场景与交互痛点（如多流程抽屉隔离、零配置本地 LocalStorage 隐私运行），AI 协助排查底层工程隐患（如 Kahn 拓扑排序环路死锁规避、React Flow 60fps 零脏重绘状态切片、AABB 空间防碰撞算法）。把涉及具体电商平台规则的业务逻辑剥离、脱敏后，我将这套底层核心开源了出来——也就是现在的 PatchCat（AI Prompt Flow Orchestrator）。

它是过去十几年里，我把底层代码、增长工程、To B/To C 业务体感与 AI 揉在一起后，真真切切从自己手里长出来的作品。现在业务流水线已经稳定运转，AI 接管了绝大部分重复劳动，我那颗喜欢从 0 到 1 折腾硬骨头的心又开始跳了——期待与气场对路的团队一起，去探索下一代 AI Native 产品的边界。

After leaving JiHu GitLab, instead of immediately jumping into another corporate role, I poured over a decade of engineering, product, and growth experience into my own cross-border private-domain e-commerce business and technical studio.

Initially, operations relied heavily on manual effort. As order volume and customer segments scaled, off-the-shelf platform tools hit a wall. I brought in AI Workflow Orchestration and AI Pair Programming: first building automated mini-programs for acquisition and community engagement, then tackling the hardest bottleneck—unifying fragmented orders, logistics, and customer identities across closed, heterogeneous platforms.

Years ago, cross-platform ETL and unified membership mapping would have required a dedicated R&D squad. Pairing with Claude, I evolved the stack step by step: starting with n8n script chains, migrating to Dify, and after hitting state-control limits in generic tools, architecting my own local visual orchestrator powered by a deterministic DAG Engine and State Machine.

Throughout this milestone co-discovery process, I defined the product ergonomics and operational scenarios (multi-flow drawer isolation, zero-config local LocalStorage privacy execution), while AI helped surface deep algorithmic edge cases (Kahn's topological cycle detection, React Flow 60fps zero-dirty-rerender state slicing, and AABB spatial collision avoidance). After stripping and anonymizing proprietary commerce rules, I open-sourced the core engine as PatchCat.

With the business pipeline now running autonomously under AI orchestration, my builder's itch to tackle new 0-to-1 challenges is back in full force.
